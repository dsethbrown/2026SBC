## Covered Skybridge Access (No Weather Exposure)

Both primary hotel venues connect directly to the OCCC West Building via covered overhead walkways — no need to go outside.

### Rosen Centre Hotel
- **Address:** 9840 International Drive (directly across from OCCC West)
- **Connection:** Rosen Centre Skywalk → OCCC West Building
- **Walking time:** ~2–3 minutes
- **Notes:** The skybridge was opened in 2011 and connects at the West Building. Most SBC breakout sessions, fellowship dinners, and ministry organization events are in this hotel's Grand Ballroom and salon spaces.

### Rosen Plaza Hotel
- **Address:** 9700 International Drive
- **Connection:** Gary Sain Memorial Skybridge → OCCC West Building
- **Walking time:** ~3–5 minutes
- **Notes:** The covered Gary Sain Memorial Skybridge provides direct OCCC access. Hosts multiple SBC organization luncheons and evening events in its ballroom spaces.

## Street-Level Walking (Outdoor, 5–15 min)

These venues are reachable on foot but involve outdoor walking along International Drive or through the OCCC campus. Use sidewalks; do not cut through parking lots after dark.

### Topgolf Orlando
- **Address:** 9295 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819
- **Walking time:** ~8 minutes
- **Route:** Exit OCCC north end → cross to Universal Blvd → Topgolf is on the left
- **Event:** Mississippi Christian University alumni gathering

### Pointe Orlando Complex
- **Address:** 9101 International Drive, Orlando, FL 32819
- **Walking time:** ~10–12 minutes
- **Route:** Walk north along International Drive sidewalk from OCCC
- **Events at this complex:**
  - **Main Event Orlando** (Suite 1032) — SC Baptist Convention Messenger Fellowship
  - **Maggiano's Little Italy** — Criswell Dinner and other organizational dinners
  - **Rodizio Grill** (Suite 1220) — Charleston Southern Alumni & Friends Dinner
- **Notes:** Pointe Orlando is an outdoor lifestyle center with covered walkways between venues once inside the complex. All three venues are within steps of each other.

### Mia's Italian Kitchen
- **Address:** 8717 International Drive, Orlando, FL 32819
- **Walking time:** ~15–18 minutes
- **Route:** Walk north along International Drive sidewalk from OCCC past Pointe Orlando
- **Events:** State Convention Regional Partners Luncheon
- **Notes:** At the northern end of the walkable I-Drive corridor from OCCC. Rideshare is a reasonable alternative if the heat is a factor.

## Inside the OCCC West Concourse

The West Concourse is a large multi-level facility. Key areas for SBC sessions:

| Area | Level | Primary Use |
|---|---|---|
| Registration / Check-in | Level 1 (lower) | Credential pickup, childcare registration |
| Hall D / Hall D1 | Level 1 | **Main Session Hall** — all plenary sessions |
| Chapin Theater | West Concourse | Large overflow sessions and special programs |
| Valencia Ballroom (North/South/East) | Level 4 | Women's Expo and large ministry events |
| Rooms 202B, 202C, 211B, 221B/C | Level 2 | Breakout session cluster (north end) |
| Rooms 224A/B/E/F, 225A/B | Level 2 | Breakout session cluster (south end) |
| Rooms 300 Lecture Hall, 304A–H, 305A | Level 3 | Breakout session cluster |
| Rooms 306A/B, 307A–D, 308A–C | Level 3 | Breakout session cluster |
| Rooms 311A–H, 312A/C, 314, 315A/B | Level 3 | Breakout session cluster |
| Room 414, 414D | Level 4 | Breakout sessions |
| North Concourse Parking Lot | Exterior | Outdoor overflow / ministry village |

**Internal orientation tips:**
- Elevators and escalators are located at both ends of the West Concourse main spine.
- Walking end-to-end on a single level takes approximately 5 minutes at a normal pace.
- Moving between adjacent levels (e.g., Level 2 → Level 3) takes approximately 2 minutes.
- Signage throughout the building uses room number prefixes as level indicators (200s = Level 2, 300s = Level 3, 400s = Level 4).

## Getting Around Orlando

### I-Ride Trolley
The International Drive Trolley runs along I-Drive and stops near the OCCC. Useful for reaching Pointe Orlando and other I-Drive restaurants without a rideshare.
- **Fare:** ~$2 per ride (exact change or pass)
- **Hours:** Typically 8 AM–10:30 PM
- **Nearest stop:** International Drive at OCCC (south end)

### Rideshare (Uber / Lyft)
- Designated pickup/drop-off zones are on the OCCC exterior, typically at the north entrance off International Drive.
- Surge pricing is common during convention start/end times — allow extra time.

### Hotel Shuttles
Check with Rosen Centre and Rosen Plaza directly about complimentary shuttle service to off-site venues during the convention period.

### Parking at OCCC
- Surface lots and structured garages are available on the OCCC campus.
- Rosen Centre and Rosen Plaza both offer self-parking with convention rates for registered guests.
- The North Concourse Parking Lot is also used for outdoor SBC ministry village activities.
