## Foreword

*Therefore I, the prisoner in the Lord, urge you to walk worthy of the calling you have received, with all humility and gentleness, with patience, bearing with one another in love, making every effort to keep the unity of the Spirit through the bond of peace.* (Ephesians 4:1–3, CSB)

In June, Southern Baptists will meet in Orlando, Florida for our Annual Meeting. Our theme this year is "Walking Worthy," which is taken from Ephesians 4:1–3.

In his epistle, Paul urges the Ephesians to "walk worthy" of the calling they have received. Worthy walk is characterized by humility, gentleness, patience, lovingly forbearing other believers, striving for the sort of unity and peace that comes from the Holy Spirit. These are the sorts of virtues and priorities that should characterize a maturing group of believers. May it be said of Southern Baptists.

When we convene in the "Theme Park Capital of the World," we want our meeting to be a gathering marked by worthy walk, in all the ways Paul commends. In Orlando, let's honor King Jesus in our worship, our witness, and the kingdom business we transact together.

This Book of Reports is a key resource for Southern Baptists as we prepare for the 2026 SBC Annual Meeting. It is tangible evidence of our twin commitments to confessional faithfulness and cooperative mission. Our various entities, boards, and commissions have shared reports about the kingdom work they've each engaged in over the past year, along with financial reports enabling messengers to see how Cooperative Program dollars are stewarded.

— Nathan A. Finn, Recording Secretary of the Southern Baptist Convention

### Significant Actions of the SBC Executive Committee

#### September 22-23, 2025 Meeting (Actions 1-11)

1. **Disability Ministry Task Force** — Approved appointment of a task force chaired by Tom Stolle in response to a 2025 motion by Benjamin Hankin.

4. **NAMB: Send Relief Puerto Rico, Inc.** — Approved NAMB's request to form a nonprofit subsidiary, conditioned on financial reporting requirements.
5. **Raymond Cutright Estate Distribution** — Allocated income distributions to the Montana Southern Baptist Convention through 2030 for pioneer missions.

8. **Standing Committee Assignments** — New EC members assigned to standing committees.
9. **Personnel Policies Manual Amendments** — Officers of the Board amended portions of the Personnel Policies Manual.
10. **Retirement Plan Changes** — Officers of the Board approved changes to the Executive Committee's retirement plan.
11. **SBC Calendar Amendments** — Adopted amendments to 2026-2030 SBC Calendars of Activities.

#### February 16-17, 2026 Meeting (Actions 12-25)

## Part 10: Governing Documents

### SBC Charter (Georgia, December 27, 1845)
The Southern Baptist Convention was incorporated by the Georgia General Assembly as "a body politic by the name and style of the Southern Baptist Convention, with authority to receive, hold, possess, retain, and dispose of property... said corporation being created for the purpose of eliciting, combining, and directing the energies of the Baptist denomination of Christians for the propagation of the gospel."

Founders: William B. Johnson, Wilson Lumpkin, James B. Taylor, A. Docrey, R.B.C. Howell, and associates.

### SBC Constitution (Selected Articles)

**Article I — The Name:** The Southern Baptist Convention.

**Article II — Purpose:** To provide a general organization for Baptists in the United States and its territories for the promotion of Christian missions at home and abroad and any other objects such as Christian education, benevolent enterprises, and social services which it may deem proper and advisable for the furtherance of the Kingdom of God.

**Article III — Composition:** The Convention shall consist of messengers who are members of Baptist churches in cooperation with the Convention. A church is deemed "in friendly cooperation" only if it:
1. Has a faith and practice closely identifying with the Convention's adopted statement of faith. (Churches which act to affirm, approve, or endorse homosexual behavior are deemed not in cooperation.)
2. Has formally approved its intention to cooperate (regular filing of annual report is one indication).
3. Has made undesignated financial contributions through the Cooperative Program and/or through the Executive Committee for Convention causes.

**Article XIV — Amendments:** Any alterations may be made to these Articles or to the adopted statement of faith at any annual meeting by a two-thirds vote of messengers, provided that an amendment is approved by two consecutive annual meetings of the Convention. *(Amendment on final approval at 2026 Annual Meeting.)*

## Part 11: Baptist Faith and Message 2000 (Selected Articles Referenced in 2026 Business)

The full BFM2000 is printed in the Book of Reports (pp. 221-227) and is available at bfm.sbc.net.

**Article VI — The Church:** The scriptural officers of the church are the pastor/elder/overseer and deacon. "The office of pastor/elder/overseer is limited to men as qualified by Scripture."

**Article XV — The Christian and the Social Order:** Christians should "contend for the sanctity of all human life from conception to natural death."

**Article XVI — Peace and War:** "The true remedy for the war spirit is the gospel of our Lord... Christians should be ready to work with all men of good will in any good cause, always being careful to act in the spirit of love without compromising their loyalty to Christ and His truth."

**Article XVII — Religious Liberty:** "God alone is Lord of the conscience, and He has left it free from the doctrines and commandments of men which are contrary to His Word or not contained in it. Church and state should be separate."

**Article XVIII — The Family:** "Marriage is the uniting of one man and one woman in covenant commitment for a lifetime."
