## Proceedings — Tuesday, June 9, 2026

1. Gerald Malloy (NC) Convention music director; worship ministry pastor, Hickory Grove Baptist Church, Charlotte, led in corporate worship.

2. Stephen Rummage (FL), executive director-treasurer, Florida Baptist Convention, Jacksonville, opened the session in prayer.

3. Jeff Iorg (TN) president and CEO, SBC Executive Committee, Nashville, presented the Bunyan Gavel to President Clint Pressley (NC) pastor, Hickory Grove Baptist Church, Charlotte.

4. President Pressley (NC) welcomed messengers to the 168th session of the Southern Baptist Convention in the 181st year of its history held at the Orange County Convention Center, Orlando, Florida.

5. President Pressley (NC) introduced the chief parliamentarian, Barry McCarty (GA) and assistant chief parliamentarian, Al Gage (AZ), along with parliamentary assistants C.J. Cavin (OK), Craig Culbreth (FL), and Kay Crews (TX). Pressley recognized Jeff Iorg (TN) who presented a commemorative plaque to Barry McCarty.

6. President Pressley (NC) called attention to the Tuesday SBC Bulletin for instructions about motions, use of digital pre-filing of motions and the following appointments: Committee on Committees, Committee on Resolutions, Registration Committee, and Tellers.

7. President Pressley (NC) recognized Tim Dowdy (GA), vice president for Evangelism, North American Mission Board, Alpharetta, for the Crossover report. Dowdy reported that 3,940 volunteers participated in the event, making 19,905 gospel presentations, resulting in 1,077 professions of faith. Participants represented 554 local churches, five local associations, and three Southern Baptist seminaries.

8. President Pressley (NC) introduced Don Currence (MO), SBC registration secretary; administrative pastor, First Baptist Church, Ozark, for the registration report and the constituting of the annual meeting. Currence reported that as of 8:22 a.m., there were 10,680 messengers. The report was adopted. Pressley recognized all first-time messengers to the Convention.

9. President Pressley (NC) recognized Bryant Sims (SC), chair, Committee on Order of Business; chief operations officer, South Carolina Baptist Convention, Columbia. Sims moved the adoption of the Convention standing rules. Jason Tolbert (AR) presented a motion to amend standing rule 6. Barry McCarty (GA) offered guidance to the messenger to clarify the motion. Tolbert spoke in favor of his motion. Bryant Sims (SC) responded on behalf of the Committee on Order of Business. Jonathan Whitehead (MO) spoke against the motion. An unidentified messenger spoke against the motion. John Piwetz (KY) spoke against the motion. Colin Smothers (KS) spoke against the motion. Chris Garner (TN) called the question. The motion to call the question passed. The amendment failed. The standing rules were adopted.

10. Bryant Sims (SC) moved the adoption of the order of business. The order of business was adopted.

11. President Pressley (NC) recognized Chaplain Major General Douglas Carver (GA), U.S. Army retired; executive director of chaplaincy, North American Mission Board, Alpharetta, led in a time of celebrating the 250th anniversary of America's Independence and American freedom. The Orange County (FL) Sheriff's Department Honor Guard presented the colors, and United States Air Force Chief of Chaplains Major General Trent Davis led messengers in the Pledge of Allegiance. Carver spoke about America's Christian heritage, the legacy of religious liberty, honored veterans and first responders, and those currently serving in the Armed Forces. United States Navy Lieutenant Chaplain Charles Daniels closed the celebration time in prayer.

12. President Pressley (NC) recognized Hunter Baker (SC), chair, Committee on Resolutions; provost and dean of faculty, North Greenville University, Greenville, to present Resolution 1: On the 250th Anniversary of the United States and the Baptist Contribution to Religious Liberty. The resolution was adopted.

13. Baker (SC) presented Resolution 2: On Appreciation for the City of Orlando. The resolution was adopted.

14. Jeff Goodyear (KY), presented Resolution 3: On Appreciation for Bivocational and Volunteer Pastors. The resolution was adopted. Jamison Reynolds (KY) spoke in favor of the resolution. Mark Clifton (KS) spoke in favor of the resolution. Goodyear asked messengers to honor bi-vocational pastors with an ovation. The resolution was adopted.

15. President Pressley (NC) recognized Cory Horton (SC) chair, Committee on Committees; senior pastor, Brushy Creek Baptist Church, Taylors, for their report. Jerome Taylor (MI) moved to correct an error in the report. The correction was approved. The report was adopted.

16. President Pressley (NC) recognized Jeff Moore (OK), chair, Committee on Nominations; senior pastor, First Baptist Church, Altus, for their report. The report was adopted.

17. President Pressley (NC) announced the time for the introduction of new motions and gave instructions.

18. Albert Mohler (KY) presented a motion: To amend Article 3, Paragraph 1 of the SBC Constitution.

19. Jonathan Greer (MS) presented a motion: To amend SBC bylaw 8, section C, subsection 3.

20. Rhett Burns (SC) presented a motion: To amend the SBC Business and Financial Plan regarding other financial contributions.

21. Tiffany Helms (SC) presented a motion: To request the SBC Executive Committee form a pastor's wives ministry task force.

22. Garrison Griffith (LA) presented a motion: To request the SBC Executive Committee form a task force to report seminary students' vocational intent.

23. Jacob Eaton (MO) presented a motion: To direct the SBC president to appoint a task force on trust, accountability and transparency in the SBC.

24. Jared Long (GA) presented a motion: To Request the SBC President appoint a study committee on women in ministry leadership.

25. Edward Willoughby (MO) presented a motion: To request a presidential task force on biblical arbitration and reconciliation.

26. Brandon Watkins (MI) presented a motion: To request a presidential task force to examine conversion to Catholicism and Eastern Orthodoxy.

27. Casey Williams (SC) presented a motion: To request a presidential task force to review and update The Baptist Faith and Message 2000.

28. President Pressley (NC) recognized D. Hance Dilbeck Jr. (TX), president and CEO, GuideStone Financial Resources, Dallas, for their report.

29. President Pressley (NC) recognized Gary Hollingsworth (TN), outgoing interim president; and Evan Lenow (TN), president, The Ethics and Religious Liberty Commission, Nashville, for their report. Will Spivy (AL) asked a question. Lenow responded. Scott Foshey (IL) asked a question. Lenow responded.

30. President Pressley (NC) recognized Joe Walker (TN), outgoing interim president and CEO; and Ryan Blackwell (TN), president and CEO, Lifeway Christian Resources, Nashville, for their report. Michael Shultz (KY) asked a question. Walker responded. Will Spivy (AL) asked a question. Walker responded.

31. President Pressley (NC) reported that the previously approved correction to the Committee on Committees report was unnecessary. He requested that messengers reverse the earlier decision and approve the original report. The original report was adopted.

32. President Pressley (NC) recognized Caleb Fleming (TX), pastor, Fairview Baptist Church, Sherman, for a Cooperative Program spotlight.

33. President Pressley (NC) recognized Daniel Ritchie (NC), First Vice President, who led the messengers in prayer.

34. Gerald Malloy (NC), Convention music director, led in corporate worship.

35. First Vice President Ritchie (NC) recognized Paul Chitwood (VA), president, International Mission Board, Richmond, for the IMB Sending Celebration during which 63 new missionaries were commissioned.

36. Gerald Malloy (NC), Convention music director, led corporate worship.

37. First Vice President Ritchie (NC) recognized President Pressley (NC), who delivered the 2026 SBC President's Address: "Walking Worthy."

38. President Pressley (NC) recognized Breck Ladd (MS) senior pastor, First Baptist Church, Madison, to close the session in prayer.

39. Gerald Malloy (NC), Convention music director, led in corporate worship.

40. Jonathan Parnell (MN), lead pastor, Cities Church, St. Paul, opened the session in prayer.

41. President Pressley (NC) recognized Adam P. Groza (CA), president, Gateway Seminary of the Southern Baptist Convention, Ontario, for their report.

42. President Pressley (NC) recognized Jason K. Allen (MO), president, Midwestern Baptist Theological Seminary, Kansas City, for their report. Rhett Burns (SC) asked a question. Allen responded.

43. President Pressley (NC) recognized James K. Dew Jr. (LA), president, New Orleans Baptist Theological Seminary, New Orleans, for their report. Will Spivy (SC) asked a question. Dew responded.

44. President Pressley (NC) recognized Daniel L. Akin (NC), president, Southeastern Baptist Theological Seminary, Wake Forest, for their report.

45. President Pressley (NC) recognized R. Albert Mohler Jr. (KY), president, The Southern Baptist Theological Seminary, Louisville, for their report. Michael Lee (NC) asked a question. Mohler responded. Juan-Jose Cavallo (FL) asked a question. Mohler responded.

46. President Pressley (NC) recognized David Dockery (TX), president, Southwestern Baptist Theological Seminary, Fort Worth, for their report. David McMillen (AL) asked a question. Jamie Dew (LA) responded because the question was directed to him rather than Dockery.

47. Lucas Taylor (KY) requested a point of personal privilege. President Pressley (NC) granted the request. Taylor asked for more seating to accommodate families that need strollers for small children. Pressley responded that the issue would be addressed for the Wednesday sessions.

48. President Pressley (NC) recognized Jasmine Lanier (FL) for a Cooperative Program spotlight which included her testimony about One More Child.

49. President Pressley (NC) announced the time for the election of president. Jay Hardwick (TN) nominated Josh Powell (SC). Adrian Taylor (FL) nominated Willy Rice (FL). Pressley (NC) recognized Don Currence (MO), registration secretary, to give balloting instructions. Currence announced 11,613 messengers were registered. Ballots were cast.

50. President Pressley (NC) announced the last opportunity for the introduction of new motions and gave instructions.

51. Gregory Mathis (KY) presented a motion: To amend SBC Bylaw 8, Section A.

52. Jared Long (GA) presented a motion: To request entities to report on alcohol policies.

53. Josh Abbotoy (TN) presented a motion: To request that the SBC Executive Committee publish a report on messengers with institutional relationships.

54. Michael Clary (KY) presented a motion: To amend SBC Bylaw 15.

55. Michael Schneider presented a motion: To request IMB provide its vaccination policy.

56. Jonathan Helvoigt (CO) presented a motion: To request NAMB to report its 10 largest payments to independent contractors.

57. Wade Thomas (KY) presented a motion: To request NAMB publish legal costs from 2020 through 2025.

58. Robbie Gibson (NC) presented a motion: To amend Article 3, Paragraph 1 of the SBC Constitution.

59. John Jones (MO) presented a motion: To request IMB to review its vaccine policies.

60. Malcolm Yarnell (TX) presented a motion: For the six seminaries and Lifeway to report on theological errors.

61. John Knudsen (MO) presented a motion: To request the SBC President form task force on messenger engagement.

62. Jeremiah Hayes (AZ) presented a motion: To request standards for worship music used at the SBC Annual Meeting.

63. Joseph Dugger (TN) presented a motion: To request the SBC Executive Committee study future annual SBC Pastors' Conference contracts.

64. Jim Cummins (TN) presented a motion: To request the SBC Executive Committee create pathways for virtual participation in the annual meeting.

65. Dean Scoular (MO) presented a motion: To request the Committee on Order of Business reframe future annual meeting schedules.

66. Brance Long (NC) presented a motion: To request the expansion of deaf ministry discipleship efforts.

67. Chase Davis (CO) made a motion to extend the time for business. The motion passed.

68. Juan-Jose Cavallo (FL) presented a motion: To condemn the rise of kinism.

69. Matt Dunn (MO) presented a motion: To form a task force to discover messengers' support of Israel.

70. John Piwetz (KY) presented a motion: To instruct the SBC Executive Committee and seminaries to more fully report expenses and operating assets.

71. Jerry Watts (MS) presented a motion: To form a task force to study responsibilities, functions, and requirements of trustees.

72. President Pressley (NC) announced the time for the election of recording secretary. Tyler Armstrong (AL) nominated Jonathan Greer (MS); Dean Inserra (FL) nominated George Schroeder (TX). Denny Burk (KY) nominated Travis Kerns (SC); Pressley called on Don Currence (MO), registration secretary, to give balloting instructions. Currence announced 11,623 messengers were registered. Ballots were cast.

73. President Pressley (NC) recognized Jeff Iorg (TN), president and CEO, SBC Executive Committee, Nashville, for a report titled "Steady Progress in the Right Direction."

74. Don Currence (MO), registration secretary, gave ballot results for the election of president. Currence reported that at the time of the ballot, there were 11,613 registered messengers; 9,063 ballots were cast: Willy Rice (FL) received 5,217 votes (57.56%); Josh Powell (SC) received 3,821 votes (42.16%); there were 25 disallowed ballots (0.28%). Pressley declared Rice (FL) as president-elect.

75. President Pressley (NC) recognized Jonathan Sams (GA) chair, Credentials Committee, for a report during the time scheduled for miscellaneous business.

76. President Pressley (NC) recognized Bryant Sims (SC), chair, Committee on Order of Business, for a report.

77. Sims (SC) reported that according to Standing Rule 6, the motion from Albert Mohler (KY), To Amend Article 3, Paragraph 1 of the Constitution, should be referred to the Executive Committee. However, because Mohler also moved for a vote to suspend Standing Rule 6, Sims recommended a vote on that matter before determining the final disposition. The motion to suspend Standing Rule 6 passed. Sims (SC) announced that the Committee on Order of Business will schedule the motion to amend for consideration during the previously scheduled business time on Wednesday morning at 8:45 a.m.

78. Sims (SC) reported that the Committee on Order of Business determined that the following motion dealt with the internal operations or ministries of the Convention entities, which under SBC Bylaw 26B shall be referred to the appropriate SBC entities. To the SBC Executive Committee: Jacob Eaton (MO), To direct the SBC president to appoint a task force on trust, accountability and transparency in the SBC.

79. Sims (SC) reported that the Committee on Order of Business determined that the following motions dealing with amendments to the statement of faith or governing documents are automatically referred to the SBC Executive Committee under the Convention standing rules: Jonathan Greer (MS) To amend SBC bylaw 8, section C, subsection 3; Rhett Burns (SC), To amend the SBC Business and Financial Plan regarding other financial contributions.

80. Sims (SC) moved on behalf of the Committee on Order of Business that the following motions be referred to the Executive Committee: Tiffany Helms (SC), To request the SBC Executive Committee form a pastor's wives ministry task force; Garrison Griffith (LA), To request the SBC Executive Committee form a task force to report seminary students' vocational intent; Jared Long (GA), To request the SBC president appoint a study committee on women in ministry leadership; Edward Willoughby (MO), To request a presidential task force on biblical arbitration and reconciliation; Casey Williams (SC), To request a presidential task force to review and update The Baptist Faith and Message 2000.

81. President Pressley (NC) recognized Ben Cole (NC) for a parliamentary inquiry. Cole spoke against the motion to refer. President Pressley (NC) recognized Jared Long (GA), who spoke against the motion to refer. Barry McCarty (GA) gave parliamentary guidance about the implications of voting against the motion to refer. The motion to refer was defeated.

82. President Pressley (NC) recognized Ben Cole (NC), who moved to postpone indefinitely all the motions in question. Sims spoke on behalf of the committee to affirm the Cole motion. Pressley recognized Jay Adkins (LA), who spoke in favor of the motion to postpone indefinitely. President Pressley (NC) recognized Joshua Daniels (GA), who moved to suspend bylaw 6 related to the Long motion. Daniels was ruled out of order. The vote to postpone the motions indefinitely passed.

83. Sims (SC) referred the motion by Brandon Watkins (MI), To request a presidential task force to examine conversion to Catholicism and Eastern Orthodoxy to the next president of the SBC. The motion was defeated. Sims (SC) moved on behalf of the committee that the motion be postponed indefinitely. The motion passed.

84. President Pressley (NC) recognized Larry Helms (SC) for an urgent request. Pressley responded.

85. Don Currence (MO), registration secretary, gave ballot results for the recording secretary. Currence reported that at the time of the ballot, there were 11,623 registered messengers; 7,809 ballots were cast: Jonathan Greer (MS) received 1,895 votes (24.27%); George Schroeder (SC) received 2,951 votes (37.79%); Travis Kerns (SC) received 2,944 votes (37.70%); there were 19 disallowed ballots (0.24%). Pressley declared a run-off between Schroeder and Kerns. Currence announced 11,627 messengers were registered. Ballots were cast.

86. President Pressley (NC) recognized Jordan Willard (NC), senior pastor, First Baptist Church, Weddington, to close the session in prayer.
