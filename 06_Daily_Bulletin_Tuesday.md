## Welcome to Orlando

Welcome to Orlando and thank you for joining us for the 2026 SBC Annual Meeting and Pastors' Conference! I pray that the Lord will be honored as we gather together to carry out the business of our Convention.

This year's theme, "Walking Worthy," comes from Ephesians 4:1, which says, "Walk in a manner worthy of the calling to which you have been called," and it is my hope and prayer that we take this to heart, pressing the joyful truths of the gospel into every part of our fellowship and deliberations.

Dr. Iorg has described our Convention as "a force for good," and I believe that to be true. As we gather over the next several days, my hope is that we will live out that truth.

Let us rejoice in the great things God has done within our churches. Let us, with resolve, stay on mission, asking that the good hand of God would remain upon us for generations to come. It has been a great joy and honor to serve as President of the Southern Baptist Convention these past two years, and I look forward to the next few days together.

*Clint Pressley, SBC President*

## Convention Standing Rules

1. A messenger may seek recognition in one of 5 ways:
   a. **For**—Allows a messenger to debate in favor of a motion or make any motion in order.
   b. **Against**—Allows a messenger to debate in opposition to a motion or make any motion in order.
   c. **Motion/Amendment**—Allows a messenger to make a new main motion, an amendment or other secondary motion such as the previous question.
   d. **Urgent Request**—Allows a messenger to raise a point of order or appeal the ruling of the chair and may only be used when a rule has been broken or the chair has made a ruling on an item.
   e. **Other**—Allows a messenger to make a motion that would normally interrupt a speaker such as a request for information, parliamentary inquiry, or question of privilege. If a messenger wishes to make one of these interrupting motions, they must relay to the page their intent to receive priority in recognition. A messenger recognized for preference may not debate or offer any other secondary motion. This may not be used to circumvent the line for debate. Please note that a request for information is a request to receive not give factual information.

2. In the Southern Baptist Convention, a motion shall be defined as calling for specific action by the Southern Baptist Convention or requesting specific action by one or more of its entities.

3. In the Southern Baptist Convention, a resolution is defined as an expression of opinion or concern at the time of adoption. Resolutions adopted by messengers during a previous Annual Meeting cannot be amended or rescinded by messengers at a subsequent Annual Meeting. However, messengers are free to adopt a new resolution to express their opinion on a given issue, including a resolution that may be contrary to one adopted by a prior Convention.

4. The time limits in the adopted program are enforceable. The start times in the adopted program do not constitute special orders for a specific time and are for guidance only.

5. A motion to call for the previous question is not in order until at least one (1) speaker "for" and one (1) speaker "against" a pending motion have been recognized.¹

6. All motions to amend the governing documents² of the Southern Baptist Convention or The Baptist Faith and Message (2000) not placed before the Convention by the Executive Committee shall be automatically referred to the Executive Committee for review and report back to the following Convention.³

7. In accordance with a long-standing custom of the Convention, affirmed by messengers in previous annual meetings, motions to censure, votes of no-confidence, and other disciplinary procedures for offenses outside the annual meeting are not in order.

---
¹ This rule may be suspended by a majority vote  
² SBC governing documents include the SBC Constitution, Bylaws, and Business Financial Plan  
³ This rule may be suspended by a majority vote

## Messenger Information

### Helpful Terminology

**MOTION**—A motion calls for specific action by the Southern Baptist Convention or requests specific action by one or more of its entities. A motion normally begins with "I move that . . . " See following page for instructions on how to make a motion.

**URGENT REQUEST**—A point of order calls the attention of the chair to a violation of the Convention's rules and should only be raised when a messenger has good reason to believe that the chair has failed to notice and correct a breach of the rules that prevents the proper transaction of business. Because a point of order interrupts pending business, it should not be raised merely to point out minor irregularities, to debate an issue, or as a method of obstructing business or harassing the chair.

**PLATFORM PERSONNEL AND COMMITTEES**—Information concerning duties of platform personnel and committees can be found in the Descriptions and Duties section below.

**RESOLUTION**—A resolution has traditionally been defined as an expression of opinion or concern, as compared to a motion, which calls for action. A resolution is not used to direct an entity of the Southern Baptist Convention to specific action other than to communicate the opinion or concern expressed. Resolutions are decided upon at the discretion of the Committee on Resolutions and can be submitted by individuals from qualified churches as early as March 15, but no later than May 15. See SBC Bylaw 20 for other requirements concerning resolutions and the Committee on Resolutions.

### To Make a Motion

1. Write your motion legibly. If the motion is spontaneous and you do not have time to write it before presenting it to the Convention at a microphone, write the motion, as you stated it, before leaving the microphone area. Motions can also be pre-filed/filed digitally (see "Pre-filing Motions").
2. Go to a floor microphone at a time when motions are being considered.
3. Notify the Page at the microphone of your desire to make a motion.
4. When your microphone is recognized by the presiding officer, state your name, the name and location of your church, and read your motion. Do not argue the merits of your motion at this time. If the motion later comes before the body for discussion, you will then be given the first opportunity to speak.
5. Provide a copy of your motion to the Recording Secretary. You can submit electronically through the SBC Annual Meeting app or by email to recordingsecretary@sbc.net, or give a typed or legibly handwritten copy on standard paper to the Page at the microphone. The Page will take the copy of your motion to the platform so the motion may be entered into the record.
6. The Committee on Order of Business will consider all presented motions and announce further action(s) to be taken, either later in the session or in a following session.

### To Speak from the Floor

1. Be at a microphone at the proper time, and make sure your desire to speak is properly characterized and input to the touch screen below the microphone at which you are standing.
2. Proceed to input your desire to speak. Do not wait for the presider to ask for input from the floor. If you are recognized for another issue, tell the chair which issue you are there to speak to, and the presider will return to you when your issue comes to the floor. The chair could use several phrases to welcome comment from the floor on any issue. If you have not entered a desire to speak on the touch screen immediately at that time (or slightly before), you will not be recognized.
3. When recognized by the chair, state your name, the fact that you are a messenger, the name and location of your church, and then voice your position and reasoning.

**Bylaws Relating to Speaking from the Floor**

- **SBC Bylaw 1C**—A messenger may speak in debate for longer than three minutes only with the permission of the Convention granted by a two-thirds vote.
- **SBC Bylaw 1D**—A messenger may introduce a second motion during a business session only if no other messenger is seeking the floor who has not made a motion during that session.

### Pre-filing Motions

You can make an important contribution to the accurate recording of our business proceedings by submitting motions or amendments electronically through the SBC Annual Meeting app or by email to recordingsecretary@sbc.net, or give a typed or legibly handwritten copy on standard paper to a page at a microphone who will then deliver to the recording secretary. For any submission, please include your name, church, and phone number.

Pre-filing does not guarantee consideration, as you are still required to go to a microphone and be recognized at the appropriate time, only then will it be entered into the official record. Pre-filing is simply a way to help with the official record and the efficiency of our work together.

## Descriptions and Duties of Roles

**THE PRESIDENT AND VICE PRESIDENTS**—The primary duty of the president is to preside during the annual session of the Convention by introducing each item of business on the agenda, recognizing speakers when they are entitled to the floor, stating and putting to a vote all questions that should come before the assembly, ruling on all questions of order, answering parliamentary inquiries relating to pending business, and assisting messengers in using correct procedure. The First and Second Vice Presidents preside in sessions at the request of the president.

**RECORDING SECRETARY**—The recording secretary is responsible for recording the proceedings of the annual session which will appear in the Wednesday Daily Bulletin as well as the SBC Annual; also serves as an ex-officio member of the SBC Executive Committee.

**REGISTRATION SECRETARY**—The registration secretary supervises the registration process, convenes the Registration Committee, reports to the Convention the number of registered messengers; responsible for the security of the ballots and tabulation of votes by the tellers; also serves as an ex-officio member of the Credentials Committee.

**EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE PRESIDENT AND CEO/TREASURER**—The President and CEO of the Executive Committee is called upon during the Executive Committee's report and at various other times during the Convention; serves as the Convention's treasurer and advises the president and other officers regarding the Convention's business; assisted on the platform by Executive Committee staff.

**PARLIAMENTARIANS**—The chief parliamentarian is responsible for advising the president on all matters of parliamentary procedure. Though the president will frequently consult the chief parliamentarian during the Convention, the parliamentarian's role is purely advisory. All rulings on questions of order are made by the president. In addition to assisting the president and the chief parliamentarian, assistant parliamentarians are available to advise the Committee on Order of Business as needed, and assist messengers with questions as they are available.

**LEGAL COUNSEL**—Outside attorneys who provide legal counsel for the president and other officers; they are also available to the Committee on Order of Business to advise in the processing of motions.

**COMMITTEE ON ORDER OF BUSINESS**—Nominated by the Committee on Nominations and elected by messengers, this committee is responsible for preparing the Convention agenda and for scheduling all matters that require a vote; the committee's chair reports several times during the Convention to inform messengers on the status of and the schedule for considering motions.

**COMMITTEE ON RESOLUTIONS**—Appointed by the SBC President, this committee proposes resolutions for consideration by messengers and presents them for consideration and/or debate during the annual meeting.

**COMMITTEE ON COMMITTEES**—Appointed by the SBC President, this committee nominates the Committee on Nominations and any special committees that are authorized during Convention sessions.

**COMMITTEE ON NOMINATIONS**—Nominated by the Committee on Committees and elected by messengers, this committee nominates members to the Executive Committee and directors/trustees to boards, institutions, commissions, and standing committees of the Convention.

**CREDENTIALS COMMITTEE**—Elected by messengers and composed of the Registration Secretary, Executive Committee chair, three members nominated by the Executive Committee, and four members nominated by the Committee on Nominations; this committee recommends whether a church is in cooperation with the Convention.

**REGISTRATION COMMITTEE**—Appointed by the SBC President, this committee supervises the registration of messengers, oversees operations of the registration desk, and rules upon any questions which may arise in registration concerning the credentials of messengers.

**TELLERS**—Appointed by the SBC President, this group tabulates the votes of messengers at the annual meeting.

**LOCAL ENCOURAGEMENT TEAM**—Appointed by the Convention Manager, this committee is mainly local to the area in and around the Annual Meeting host city; they support the Annual Meeting by coordinating volunteers, hosting the prayer room, and making connections within the host city.

## Disposition of Resolutions Submitted to the 2026 Committee on Resolutions

> **Note:** The time for consideration of a motion to bring out a resolution declined by the committee is on Wednesday afternoon at the conclusion of the Committee on Resolutions Report (Third).

1. **On the Unity of the Office, Function, and Gifting of Pastor/Shepherd/Elder/Overseer** — Jonathan Greer, Pascagoula, MS  
   *See Resolution 9, "On the Office and Function of Pastor/Elder/Overseer"*

2. **On the Use and Development of a More Trusted, Biblically Based Artificial Intelligence Program for use by Christians Everywhere** — Mel Howton, Steens, MS  
   *Declined.* While the Committee affirms the importance of Christian discernment in the development and use of artificial intelligence, this topic was addressed in the 2023 resolution "On Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Technologies," and we believe that resolution continues to speak adequately to the concerns raised here. This resolution is also more in the form of a motion as it calls for action instead of offering a particular opinion of the Convention.

3. **Honoring the Semiquincentennial Anniversary** — David Henneke, Kingsland, TX  
   *See Resolution 1, "On the 250th Anniversary of the United States and the Baptist Contribution to Religious Liberty"*

4. **On Expressing Appreciation for Creeds** — David Henneke, Kingsland, TX  
   *Declined.* While the Committee affirms the historic value of Christian creeds as expressions of orthodox doctrine and their importance to Baptist identity, this topic was substantially addressed in the 2025 resolution "On Honoring the Centennial Anniversary of The Baptist Faith and Message (1925) and Celebrating the 25th Anniversary of the Baptist Faith and Message (2000)," which specifically affirmed the Nicene Creed and our confessional heritage.

5. **On SBC Neutrality Concerning Theories of the Substitutionary Work of Christ** — Tommy Lynn Whedbee, Mansfield, AR  
   *Declined.* The Committee does not believe it is appropriate for the Convention to adopt a posture of neutrality on matters addressed by our confessional statement. The Baptist Faith and Message 2000 affirms the substitutionary atonement of Christ as central to our soteriology, and we believe our confession speaks clearly to the concerns raised in this resolution.

6. **On "Digital or Virtual Church"** — Steven Head, Dover, TN

7. **On Holding Both Men and Women Equally Accountable for Abortion** — Philip Bramblet, Rocky Mount, VA  
   *Declined.* The Committee does not believe there is sufficient consensus among Southern Baptists on the question of criminal proceedings regarding abortion. We would refer messengers to the 2021 resolution "On Abolishing Abortion" and the 2022 resolution "On Anticipating a Historic Moment in the Pro-Life Movement," which remain recent and adequate expressions of our Convention's pro-life convictions.

8. **On Honoring the Life and Public Witness of Charlie Kirk** — Chris Garner, Bells, TN  
   *Declined.* While the Committee recognizes that many Southern Baptists have been encouraged by the public work of Mr. Kirk, we do not believe it is within the purview of the Committee—nor is it customary practice—to bring forward resolutions honoring public figures who are not Southern Baptist.

9. **On Calling Southern Baptists to Cease Litigation and Pursue Biblical Reconciliation** — Howard Harden, Homestead, FL  
   *Declined.* While the Committee affirms the biblical mandate for reconciliation among believers and acknowledges that Scripture calls the church to pursue peace, we do not believe this topic merits a resolution this year.

## Additional Information

### Special Seating

There is reserved seating located in the Convention Hall for those with disabilities, parents with strollers, and those hard of hearing. To locate how to get to these designated sections, please see an usher.

### Food Options

There are multiple concession stands located inside the Orange County Convention Center as well as many restaurants within walking distance of the Convention Center. In addition to the food stands located in the Convention Center hallways and common areas, there are food vendors set up in the Exhibit Hall as well as Hall C. Convention Center dining information as well as an Orlando dining guide are available in the SBC Annual Meeting app.

### Annual Meeting Hashtag

**#SBC26**

Use the official SBC Annual Meeting hashtag on your social media messages and photos concerning the 2026 SBC Annual Meeting and stay connected with everything that is happening.

### Childcare

- Registration for the preschool and children programs took place earlier in the spring, prior to the Annual Meeting. In order to ensure the proper worker-to-child ratios, no on-site registrations will be accepted.
- Each year, we are able to offer the programs during Annual Meeting week for registered children. This year, drop-off and pick-up will happen on the lower level of the Orange County Convention Center next to registration.
- A nursing mothers' room is also available for mothers whose babies aren't registered in childcare. Visit the Preschool check-in area for directions. Please consult the Information Desk for more information.
