# 2026 SBC Annual Meeting — Exhibitor Directory

**277 exhibitors** on the convention exhibit floor at the Orange County Convention Center (OCCC), Orlando, FL.  
For venue-level navigation (hotels, skybridge, rideshare, OCCC room locations), see [04_Venue_Navigation.md](04_Venue_Navigation.md).

> Booth positions are derived from the official [SBC 2026 floor plan](https://sbc26.mapyourshow.com/8_0/floorplan/index.cfm?hallID=campus&level=2&st=exhibitor). Section groupings reflect physical floor-plan coordinates. Walking estimates are relative to the main exhibit hall entrance and the timing baseline in the navigation guide (~5 min end-to-end on a single OCCC level).

---

## Exhibit Hall Overview

All booths are in **Hall A** of the OCCC West Concourse. The floor divides into two physically distinct areas:

| Area | Booths | Key Landmarks | Est. from Exhibit Entrance |
|------|--------|---------------|----------------------------|
| **West Wing** | 57 | SWBTS, NOBTS, Lifeway (550), Texas Baptists, WMU (1214) | Separate section — allow extra time |
| **Main Hall — Entrance End** | 45 | Mid-America Seminary, Samford, Sweetwater | Near entrance (~1–2 min) |
| **Main Hall — Center-West** | 27 | GuideStone (800), SBC Executive Committee (830), ERLC (638) | ~3–4 min |
| **Main Hall — Center-East** | 45 | NAMB (1000), Send Relief (1014), IMB (1024), Lifeway (1271), WMU (1211) | ~5–6 min |
| **Main Hall — Far End** | 103 | Gateway Seminary (1400), Billy Graham Assoc. (1846), SBC Worship Stage (466\*) | ~7–9 min |

\* *Booth 466 (SBC Worship Stage) hosts live worship sets — see [2026_SBC_Annual_Meeting_Events.md](2026_SBC_Annual_Meeting_Events.md) for performance schedule.*

**West Wing note:** The West Wing coordinates in the floor plan place this section in a separate hall cluster. It contains major denominational entities (SWBTS, NOBTS, Lifeway, Texas Baptists) and likely represents an adjacent connected hall or annex rather than the outdoor North Concourse area. Budget a few extra minutes to locate the connecting corridor.
